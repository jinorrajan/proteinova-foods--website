Master Privacy Policy & Mobile App Data Safety Notice

Last Revised: October 2026 | Covers Proteinova Web Services & Proteinova Connect Android App

1 Preamble & Regulatory Framework

Proteinova Food Products Private Limited (hereinafter referred to as “Proteinova”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the official web portal at proteinova.in, associated supply portals, and the Proteinova Connect Android mobile application.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and transmission of personal and operational data collected from retail consumers, wholesale B2B partners, branch managers, delivery operators, and app users. It complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Google Play Developer Distribution Policies.

2 Information We Collect in the App and Web Services

We collect only information strictly necessary for poultry farm distribution, inventory branch transfers, order fulfillment, and operational safety:

A. Account & Profile Information: Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, corporate branch assignment, and role credentials created for authorized branch and wholesale access.

B. Commercial & Logistics Data: Goods & Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN), FSSAI license numbers, dispatch depot locations, branch transfer records, egg crate batches, and consignment receipts.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry: Device model, operating system version, app build version, IP address, connection status, crash logs, and performance metrics required to ensure smooth operational uptime.

3 Mobile Device Permissions Requested (Proteinova Connect)

The Proteinova Connect Android application may request access to specific device features solely to fulfill core operational functions:

📷 Camera Access
Used exclusively for optical barcode/QR scanning of egg crates and uploading physical delivery verification photos.
📍 Location (Coarse / Fine)
Used for verifying arrival at authorized branch depots and optimizing cold-chain refrigerated delivery route transfers.
💾 Storage / Files
Used to download, store, and view PDF tax invoices, dispatch slips, and inspection audit certificates.
🌐 Internet & Network State
Required to synchronize stock levels, verify active login tokens, and update real-time branch transfer hub records.

* Note: You may grant or deny permissions via your Android system settings. Denying permissions will not break unrelated features.

4 Purpose of Data Processing

Data collected through Proteinova Connect and web systems is used exclusively for:

  • Facilitating scheduled refrigerated deliveries, order tracking, and farm-gate supply operations.
  • Managing branch-to-branch egg transfers, stock audits, and inventory reconciliation.
  • Ensuring farm-to-table food safety traceability in strict adherence to statutory FSSAI and ISO 22000 quality mandates.
  • Detecting, preventing, and troubleshooting crashes or fraudulent system operations.

5 Zero Sale of Personal Data & Third-Party Disclosure

Proteinova maintains an absolute zero-monetization policy regarding user data. We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal or operational data to third-party advertising brokers or data aggregators.

Information is shared only under strict operational confidentiality with:

  • Authorized Cold-Chain Logistics Operators: Exclusively for completing physical dispatches and deliveries.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Services: Enterprise cloud hosts utilizing ISO/IEC 27001-compliant TLS encryption.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Authorities: When legally mandated by judicial subpoenas or food safety regulatory audits under Indian law.

6 Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain operational data only as long as necessary to provide service, maintain financial audit logs under Indian tax regulations, and fulfill FSSAI traceability requirements.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
Users may request the deletion of their user profile and associated personal data at any time by emailing our Data Protection Officer at compliance@proteinovafoods.com with the subject line "Account / Data Deletion Request". Requests are verified and fulfilled within 30 statutory business days.

7 Security Safeguards

All data in transit across our Android mobile app and web services is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Access to administrative tools is gated behind role-based access control (RBAC) and monitored for security compliance.

8 Children’s Privacy

Proteinova Connect and our commercial supply portals are directed toward adults, commercial wholesale partners, and authorized staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
